What I'm working on

  • Launched 2026

    Culture Finance Standard

    Climate has its standards. Social has its measurement. Cultural value is the largest unmeasured category in sustainability finance — so we're building the third.

  • Funds in the field

    Innovative finance for culture

    The Feyha Çelenk Theatre Entrepreneurship Support Program in Bursa — a funding model for independent performing arts productions, built with BKSTV and Alethina Impact. And the Bozcaada Viticulture Fund, applying the same thinking to a 4,000-year-old heritage on the island. Both are still in progress, with more on the way.

  • 10th edition · 2026

    Bozcaada Jazz Festival & KEŞİF

    Three stages, 50+ music happenings, and pop-up concerts across the island. In 2023 we ran the first festival impact measurement of its kind in Türkiye; the second chapter is underway.

    KEŞİF is the gathering format we created around it — for the conversations the music alone can't hold. Recent tables have brought together UN Women, Google Türkiye, the Turkish Entrepreneurship Foundation, impact investors, and the island's own producers.

About

I've spent over two decades at the intersection of live music, cultural production, and building companies. It started with collecting a bag at Istanbul Airport at 4 am for a visiting artist — and it hasn't slowed down since.

Festivals, companies, labels, funds. Some thrived. Some were wound down with care. All of them were worth doing.

I'm based in Berlin, but I travel a lot between Europe and Türkiye. Through 3dots, my work now sits where policy, ecosystem building and innovative finance meet — designing the conditions for culture to be properly funded, rather than perpetually rescued.

Away from the desk

Black and white analog photography, whenever I find the time — two decades of it now, and I still feel like an amateur. I don't know much about it. I just like doing it.

An adventure bike, and a trip somewhere with the same friend every year. Collecting sounds, and making music that mostly never gets released. And a standing wish for more time at sea, with a boat.

Milestones

  • Since 2018

    3dots

    Co-Founder

    3dots is the long-running vehicle through which I've co-founded and supported multiple projects at the intersection of culture, impact and entrepreneurship. What started as a project company has become an ecosystem builder — connecting founders, resources, and financial instruments across the creative and cultural industries.

    Projects launched or co-founded through 3dots:

    Bozcaada Jazz Festival — reaching its 10th edition in 2026.

    XJAZZ Istanbul, Ankara, İzmir — regional expansions of the XJAZZ concept.

    Bergama Theatre Festival — built and exited in 2025, with deep pride in what it became. Still a fan, and a curator of the Akropol events in its closing chapter.

    Space Goats (label) — launched and wound down. Couldn't find its footing.

    Lycia Records — ongoing.

    Kabak & Lin Records — formed from a merger with Lin Records; exited.

    Erişilebilir Her Şey — an accessibility consultancy co-founded to help cultural operators adopt accessibility principles. Exited after formation due to structural changes. Still a big fan, watching it grow from the outside.

  • Since 2021

    Türkiye Caz Ağı

    Founder · Türkiye Jazz Network

    Founded on the model of Europe Jazz Network, to connect the country's festivals, venues and institutions into something that could act together rather than separately. Members include Istanbul Jazz Festival, Akbank Jazz Festival, Babylon, Borusan Müzik Evi and Arter.

  • 2021 – 2024

    Deplike

    Co-Founder · Music Tech · London & Ankara

    My first real step into tech — something I'd wanted for a long time. Deplike is a music technology company. I came in as co-founder, raised seed investment during an economic crisis, and oversaw global partnerships. Left in 2024 to focus on what was building at 3dots.

    Crossing from cultural production into a tech startup was a significant shift.

  • 2018

    XJAZZ Berlin

    Co-Founder & Co-CEO · Still a Shareholder

    Co-founded and co-ran XJAZZ out of Berlin, building it into an international festival format. Editions ran in Türkiye, Belarus, and Sweden (the latter cancelled due to COVID), alongside the main event in Germany.

    I remain a shareholder — with full support and love for what it stands for.

  • 2015

    Sónar Istanbul

    Initiator & Deal Maker · Barcelona Franchise · Istanbul

    It started as a question from my sister, Funda — Murat, have you heard of Sónar Barcelona? Two years of groundwork later, while I was still working part-time at Charmenko, the deal was closed and delivered in 2017. Bringing the iconic Barcelona franchise to Türkiye for the first time required a specific combination of relationships, persistence, and timing.

    The festival has continued running in Istanbul since. I'm no longer involved, but proud to have been the one who made it happen.

  • 2015

    Moved to Berlin

    A deliberate shift. Berlin in 2015 was one of the world's most important cities for music, independent culture, and the kind of creative economy I wanted to build in.

  • 2010 – 2014

    Pozitif Live

    Projects & Production

    My relationship with Pozitif stretches back to 2004 and runs through to 2014 — a decade in total, though in different capacities. The 2010–2014 period was the most concentrated: office-based work in the projects and production department, with some years part-time alongside other commitments. The work covered the full range of what Turkish live music looked like at its peak.

    Projects included Rock'n Coke, Cirque du Soleil's Saltimbanco and Alegria Turkish runs, Efes Pilsen One Love Festival, Efes Pilsen Blues Festival, Akbank Jazz Festival, and major one-off concerts — including U2's Istanbul show and Red Hot Chili Peppers' Istanbul show.

  • 2008 – 2015

    Charmenko / Charm Music

    Türkiye Projects Manager & Artist Booker · Part-time through 2015

    Moved from the production floor into booking — working across major and emerging acts in Türkiye and Eastern European markets. This was where freelance became professional: managing artist relationships, negotiations, and touring projects at regional scale. Continued in a part-time capacity after 2010, which is how the groundwork for Sónar Istanbul began taking shape around 2015.

  • 2003 – 2008

    The Istanbul Years — Formation

    Freelance · Artist Guide, Production, Coordination

    It started at 4 am at Istanbul Airport, collecting a bag for a visiting artist for the Istanbul Film Festival. Over the next five years I took part in over 300 events across the city — as artist guide, production assistant, coordinator, and in whatever role needed filling.

    The range was everything Istanbul had at the time: Istanbul Film Festival, Istanbul Jazz Festival, Babylon, IF Istanbul, Akbank Jazz Festival, and music events across genres. This period was less a job and more a complete education in how live culture actually works — from the artist's perspective, the crew's, and the audience's.
